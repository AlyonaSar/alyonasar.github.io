ABOUT

Alyona Antonova

I am interested in expanding human intelligence through technology. AI, robots and new ways of working with information can help people solve problems, remember things and learn significantly faster. I want to understand how to make this transition convenient and safe for people.

Currently

I study Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at ITMO University, specializing in Mechatronics. I work on technical and research projects, teach programming in person, tutor students online and develop my own initiatives.

How I work

I am comfortable working across roles: understanding the technical details, organizing the process, bringing people together, building a prototype or infrastructure and presenting the result.

Tools

Python, OpenCV, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, NumPy, pandas, MATLAB, Java, basic C/C++, Linux, Git, LaTeX, Obsidian, Syncthing, Automate, AutoHotkey, GPT Pro, TickTick, ingenuity and creativity.

Interests

Robotics, computer vision, R&D, quantum technologies, automation, education and technology entrepreneurship.

What I organize

With Maxim Gilyov, I organize Obsidian workshops, both private sessions for the Future Leaders Foundation and public events.

I organize Human and AI hackathons and city hikes in Saint Petersburg. As part of Geekmo, I also help organize the annual Russian Railways ball.

I enjoy bringing people together around ideas and creating events where they can try something new.
