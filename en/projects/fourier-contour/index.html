PROJECT / 07 · 2025

Drawing Images with Fourier Vectors

A Python program turns an image contour into a GIF drawn by rotating Fourier vectors.

MY ROLE

Author · software development

01

Concept

Represent a complex two-dimensional shape as a sum of rotating vectors.

02

Mathematics

Fourier coefficients determine the amplitudes and phases of the vectors approximating the contour.

03

Visualization

The endpoint traces the shape as the vectors rotate. The animation can be exported as a GIF.

04

Result

The program combines image processing, mathematical approximation and animation generation.

Try it yourself

Upload a high-contrast image or use the example. A single closed contour works best: the longest outer contour is selected, and separate details and inner contours are omitted. Image processing and GIF generation happen locally in your browser; your image is not uploaded.
